Why use a local Squirrel?

Professional photo scanners can cost hundreds of dollars, and a large collection takes real time to prepare, scan, review, organize, and deliver properly. A local Squirrel already has the equipment and the experience.

✓ Local pickup and return
✓ Price agreed before work begins
✓ Originals remain nearby
✓ Scanned and organized properly

Scan Squirrel began after the Los Angeles fires highlighted how quickly family photographs and personal history can disappear. The project is being built to give families practical tools, free guidance, and access to trusted local people who can help preserve their memories.
